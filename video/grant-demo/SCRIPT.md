# Optional voiceover

Not in the rendered cut. The film is text-led. These lines are written for a 2:02 read
if the team records a voice later. Each section starts at the scene time and leaves room
to breathe.

## 0:00 Hook

A price is not a license. It is a list of limits, and nobody shows you where they are.

## 0:11 Reveal

Grant is a music licensing checkout for editors. It shows the limits before you pay.

## 0:24 Brief and shortlist

You describe the cut. Grant turns it into terms, ranks the catalogue against them, and
gives every match a reason you can check.

## 0:39 Strip

Six checks, one strip. Platform, term, territory, client delivery, reposting, future
campaigns. Filled is covered. Hollow is not.

## 0:55 Refusal

Ask for paid ads on a track that does not offer them and Grant refuses before anything is
signed. No reservation. No receipt. No surprise.

## 1:10 Settlement

When the terms fit, the payment settles on Stellar testnet and the license arrives with
the PDF, the audio, and a public proof page.

## 1:28 Agent

The seller publishes its terms over x402. The buying agent reads them, refuses before
signing when the brief does not fit, and pays once when it does.

## 1:43 Stack

The same code runs the live demo: Stellar, x402, USDC, Neon, R2, and the app.

## 1:53 Close

Grant. Know what the license covers before you export.
