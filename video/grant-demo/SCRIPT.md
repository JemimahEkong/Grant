# Voiceover

Recorded with Edge TTS, voice `en-US-AvaNeural`, pitch -2Hz. The rate is chosen per scene
so each block fits its window. Generated by `scripts/make-vo.py` into
`assets/audio/vo.mp3` at 48 kHz stereo, 122 seconds.

## 0:00 Hook

A music license is more than a price. It's a set of limits, and a line you only find
after you export.

## 0:11 Reveal

Grant is a music checkout for freelance editors. It shows what a track's license actually
allows, before you approve. One screen. Six checks.

## 0:24 Brief and shortlist

You describe the cut. The platforms, the term, the territory. Grant turns that into terms,
then ranks the catalogue against them. Eight tracks. Every reason comes from the offer.

## 0:39 Strip

Every quote is six checks. Platform, term, territory, client, reposting, future. Filled is
covered. Hollow is not. Organic coverage does not include paid ads.

## 0:55 Refusal

Ask for paid ads on a track that doesn't offer them, and Grant refuses the quote. No
signature. No reservation. No receipt. A refusal is cheaper than a bad license.

## 1:10 Settlement

When the terms fit, the payment settles on Stellar testnet. One US dollar, from buyer to
seller. Then the license, the audio, and a public proof page. Real testnet settlement. No
real money.

## 1:28 Agent

The seller publishes its terms over x402. The agent reads them, refuses before signing if
the brief doesn't fit, and pays once when it does. Two refusals. One payment.

## 1:43 Stack

Live demo, same code. Next.js, Neon, Stellar, x402.

## 1:53 Close

Grant. Know what the license covers before you export.
